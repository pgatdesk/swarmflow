### 10-replication-loss

Required scenario 10: s1 keeps 3 verified copies (-replicas 3) on replica holders r1-r3. r1 is killed at 12 s (copies lost, must be re-created on r2/r3). The original seeder leaves (graceful stop) at 17 s and a downloader joins at 18 s, so the download can only complete from the replicas.

File 32MiB in 256KiB pieces. Each peer process runs in its own network namespace, with its access link shaped by `tc netem` in both directions (decision 0005). Trials: 5 per variant, interleaved. Values are median [p25–p75] across trials.
