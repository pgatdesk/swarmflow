### selection-heterogeneous

Peer-selection comparison. 10 seeds with very different upload rate (2–100 Mbit/s) and one-way delay (5–200 ms) serve 2 downloaders on fast links. With 10 seeds × 4 requests per peer = 40 slots against a 32-request budget, the selector has real choices to make. Identical workload and seeds for every variant; only -selector differs.

File 64MiB in 256KiB pieces. Each peer process runs in its own network namespace, with its access link shaped by `tc netem` in both directions (decision 0005). Trials: 5 per variant, interleaved. Values are median [p25–p75] across trials.
