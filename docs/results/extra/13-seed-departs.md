## Network experiment: 13-seed-departs

Generated by `swarmlab netem -scenario scenarios/extra/13-seed-departs.json -trials 5` on 2026-10-26 (go1.27.0, 16 CPUs), 25m3s total. Measured with real processes and kernel traffic shaping on one host.

### 13-seed-departs

Extra (validates decision 0003 on a real network): one 40 Mbit/s seed and eight downloaders with 10 Mbit/s uplinks. The seed leaves after 20 s (about 1.5 copies uploaded); downloaders can only finish if, between them, they already hold every piece. In simulation, without the upload admission cap, no downloader finished.

File 64MiB in 256KiB pieces. Each peer process runs in its own network namespace, with its access link shaped by `tc netem` in both directions (decision 0005). Trials: 5 per variant, interleaved. Values are median [p25–p75] across trials.
