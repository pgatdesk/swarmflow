### selection-churn

As selection-heterogeneous, but the fastest seed (s1) is killed with SIGKILL 3 s in, and the second-fastest (s2) loses its link from 3 s to 6 s (link down, then back up: a transient failure). Measures whether each selector recovers when its preferred peers fail.

File 64MiB in 256KiB pieces. Each peer process runs in its own network namespace, with its access link shaped by `tc netem` in both directions (decision 0005). Trials: 5 per variant, interleaved. Values are median [p25–p75] across trials.
