### selection-flaky

As selection-heterogeneous, but the fastest seed (s1) is flaky: its link goes down for 2.5 s at 2 s, 6 s and 10 s (up again at 4.5 s, 8.5 s, 12.5 s). When up it is the best source, so its measured throughput stays high; only timeouts and reliability reveal the outages. This is the condition the adaptive selector's reliability term targets.
